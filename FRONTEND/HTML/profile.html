About Us
Our Vision

To deliver a structured, data-driven, and user-centric platform that supports electric vehicle travel through accurate battery forecasting, intelligent route optimization, and real-time decision support.

Core Features
  • Battery Forecasting & Range Prediction

    Estimates remaining battery level, time-to-empty, and arrival state of charge based on vehicle specifications, user inputs, and environmental factors.

  • Route-Based Energy Estimation

    Calculates expected battery usage for selected destinations using route distance and driving assumptions.

  • Charging Support & Trip Planning

    Identifies nearby charging stations and assists users in planning trips to reduce range anxiety.

  • Safety & Trip Awareness

    Provides low-battery warnings and trip status indicators to support safer electric vehicle journeys.

  • Sustainability Insights

    Presents energy usage and environmental impact metrics to encourage efficient and responsible EV use.
